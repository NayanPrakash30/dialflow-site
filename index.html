Installing DialFlow

Why DialFlow isn't on the Play Store

It's about policy and cost, not quality. Here's the honest version, plus how to check that your download is the real thing.

A policy and cost thing, not a quality thing

Google Play's rules around apps that place calls automatically are restrictive, and listing there means a paid developer account plus Google's billing cut on every payment. Both would change how DialFlow works, or push the price up. So instead of bending the app to fit a store, it ships to you directly as a signed Android app.

Direct downloads are normal for small Android utilities. What matters is where you get the file and whether it's properly signed. On both counts, DialFlow is built to be checked.

  • Signed & tamper-checked

    Every build is signed with our private key. Android refuses to install an update that isn't genuinely ours, so a modified copy can't quietly replace the real app.

  • Play Protect still scans it

    Android's built-in Google Play Protect scans DialFlow when you install it and keeps watching it afterwards. Installing outside the store doesn't remove that safety net.

  • One official source

    Download DialFlow only from this page. If a build didn't come from here, it isn't ours — don't install it.

  • Nothing to harvest

    DialFlow has no contacts or call-log access at all. The only numbers it ever knows are the ones you paste into it.

Installing takes about a minute

  1. Download the APK with the button at the top of this page.
  2. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this one file — a settings shortcut appears on the prompt.
  3. Open DialFlow and grant the Phone permission. That is what lets it place a call and notice when the call has ended.
  4. Paste your numbers and press Start. Your trial begins on first launch.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

No import wizard, no account, no configuration file.

  1. 1

    Paste your list

    Copy your numbers from wherever they live — a note, a spreadsheet, a CRM export — and paste them in. DialFlow pulls out the numbers and ignores the rest.

  2. 2

    Choose the gap

    Pick 3, 5 or 10 seconds, or type your own. Grant the Phone permission once — that is what lets the app place calls and detect when a call has ended.

  3. 3

    Press Start and work the list

    Rows update live as each call is placed and finished. Pause when you need to talk, stop when you're done, and pick up later where you left off.

What happens on each call

  1. DialFlow places the call to the next number on the list.
  2. It watches the phone's call state until the call actually connects.
  3. It waits for the call to end — you talk for as long as you need.
  4. It marks that row called, counts out your gap, then moves to the following number.

If a number doesn't connect within about 25 seconds, the row is marked and DialFlow moves on rather than hanging on a dead line.

Straight answers

What DialFlow doesn't do

It's better to know this before you pay than after.

  • It doesn't know who picks up. It dials on a timer, so it can't tell a voicemail, a busy tone or a wrong number from a real conversation.
  • It doesn't make internet calls — no WhatsApp, Telegram or VoIP. Regular SIM calls only, one SIM at a time.
  • It never reads your contacts, so the list is exactly what you paste in. Nothing is dialed that you didn't put there.
  • One licence covers one device, matched to the device code. We can move it to a new phone if you replace yours.
  • It needs the Phone permission to work. Without it, the app can't place calls at all.
  • Use it for people who expect your call. Automatic dialing is regulated in some places, and honouring do-not-call requests and consent is your responsibility.
Questions

The things people ask before buying

Is the trial really free?

Yes. 24 hours of full use from the first time you open the app. No card, no account, nothing to cancel — it simply ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

The Start button stops and the app asks for a licence key. Your list, your gap setting and everything else stay exactly as they were. Paste a key and you carry on from the same place.

How do I get a licence key?

Pay by UPI, then send us the screenshot along with your device code on WhatsApp or email. We issue the key by hand, usually within a few hours. It's a short string starting with DLF1. — paste it into the app and DialFlow unlocks.

Can I use one key on two phones?

No — each key is issued for one device code. That's what stops a key from being passed around. If you change phones, message us and we'll move your licence across.

Does it read my contacts or call log?

No. DialFlow has no contacts permission and no call-log permission. It reads only the phone's live call state (idle, ringing, off-hook) so it knows when to dial the next number. Your numbers come only from what you paste.

Is my number list uploaded anywhere?

No. The list, your settings and your licence are stored on your phone. There's no server holding them and no analytics on what you dial.

What if a call doesn't connect?

DialFlow waits about 25 seconds for the call to connect. If it doesn't, the row is marked and it moves on, so one dead number can't stall the whole list.

Can I pause in the middle of a session?

Yes. Pause holds the list where it is, resume carries on from the same number, and stop ends the session — the rows already dialed keep their status.

Will it work on my phone?

Any Android 8.0 or newer phone. Dual-SIM phones work too, though each call uses the SIM your phone normally dials with.

Is downloading an APK safe?

An APK from the right place, properly signed, is the same file a store would give you. DialFlow is signed with its own key, Play Protect scans it on install, and we publish a SHA-256 fingerprint so you can confirm the file wasn't modified. Get it only from this page.

Refunds?

A licence key is delivered digitally and immediately, so payments can't be refunded once a key has been sent. If your key doesn't work we'll fix or replace it — just message us. Details are in the refund policy.
